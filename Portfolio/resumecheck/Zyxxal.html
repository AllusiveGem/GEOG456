Prideful. If outsmarted, he becomes reckless (loses Legendary Resistance for 1 round).

Actions

Multiattack. Zyxxal makes two Deceitful Bolt attacks or uses Mocking Mirage once.

Deceitful Bolt. Ranged Spell Attack: +10 to hit, range 120 ft.
Hit: 22 (4d8 + 6) psychic damage, and the target must make a DC 18 Wisdom save or be charmed for 1 minute (repeat save at end of turns).

Mocking Mirage (Recharge 5-6). Zyxxal creates three illusory duplicates within 30 feet. Attacks against him have disadvantage unless the attacker first succeeds on a DC 18 Investigation check.

Warp Reality (Costs 2 Actions). Zyxxal warps space in a 30-foot cube. Each creature inside must make a DC 18 Dexterity save or be teleported 30 feet in a random direction (possibly into hazards).

Misdirection. Swap places with an illusion/ally (60 ft.).

Zyxxal's Lair: The Carnival of Shattered Minds

A writhing, neon-lit funhouse where the air smells of burning sugar and the distant calliope plays backwards. Guests (victims) stagger through attractions that warp their senses, never sure what's real.

Lair Actions (On Initiative 20)

The Tunnel of "Love"

A 30-foot-long tunnel of strobing, fleshy mirrors erupts from the ground. Creatures inside must make a DC 18 CON save or be poisoned (vomiting rainbow sludge) and see all allies as Zyxxal's grinning duplicates for 1 round.

Carousel of Flesh

The carnival's carousel spins violently, hurling razor-edged wooden horses in a 20-foot radius. Creatures take 4d6 slashing damage (DC 18 DEX save for half). Those who fail are grappled by a horse's jagged teeth (escape DC 18).

The Big Top's Curtain Call

The circus tent's flaps lurch to life, attempting to swallow a creature (60 ft. range). DC 18 STR save or be restrained inside the tent's "throat," taking 3d10 acid damage (digested by the carnival itself) per round.

Cotton Candy Cocaine

Pink, floating strands of "cotton candy" burst near a creature's face. They must make a DC 18 WIS save or be addicted:

Addiction: Must spend each move action eating more or take 2d12 psychic withdrawal damage. Lasts until lesser restoration or Zyxxal dies.

Regional Effects (1 Mile Radius)

  • Clowns in the Corners of Your Vision. NPCs see pale-faced jesters (that vanish when stared at) whispering terrible ideas.
  • The Ground Breathes. Roads and paths undulate like a living thing, slowing travel speed by half.
  • Food is Alive. Every meal has a 50% chance to scream, bite, or beg for mercy when eaten.
  • Time is a Suggestion. Clocks run backwards, and NPCs remember events that haven't happened yet.

Carnival Attractions (Environmental Hazards)

The Hall of a Thousand Grins

Mirrors show visitors their deaths. DC 15 WIS save or gain a short-term madness effect.

The Ferris Wheel to Nowhere

Riders disappear at the top, reappearing 1d4 hours later with no memory or extra limbs.

The Hammer Test of Fate

A "Test Your Strength" game that actually steals STR (1d4 STR damage on a failed DC 18 CON save).

Roleplaying Zyxxal in the Carnival

Voice: A mix of Hunter S. Thompson and the Joker. "You bought the ticket, now take the RIDE, baby! HaHA!"

Tactics:

  • Rides the carousel horses like a cavalry charge.
  • Forces PCs to play rigged carnival games to progress (e.g., "Shoot the cleric's hat off to win!").
  • Offers "prizes" (cursed items) for "winning."

Weakness: The carnival's chaos is tied to his focus—if PCs destroy the central calliope (AC 15, 50 HP), lair actions stop.

Example Twisted Wish in the Carnival

Wish: "I want to win this game!"

Curse: The rigged game awards a "prize"—the PC's own severed hand, which now crawls after them, mocking them in Zyxxal's voice.

NPC: The Mirage Dealer

"Doc Phantasm" – A shimmering, skeletal figure in a stained lab coat, selling "Fey Dust" from a floating cart.

AC 12 | HP 1 (illusion) | Speed 0 ft. (hovers)

Abilities:

Psychedelic Pitch: As an action, offers a PC "Fey Dust" (DC 18 CHA save or compelled to take it).

Fey Dust Effects (1d4):

  1. Euphoria: Gain advantage on CHA checks for 1 hour, then disadvantage on WIS saves for 1d4 days.
  2. Bad Trip: Hallucinate swarms of spiders (treat as confusion spell for 1 min).
  3. Reality Slip: Teleport 1d6 × 10 ft. randomly each round for 1 min.
  4. Zyxxal's Gaze: User sees Zyxxal's face in every reflection (no mechanical effect... yet).

Tactics: Only Zyxxal can kill Doc Phantasm (it's his hallucination). Destroying the cart just makes it reappear.

Combat Flow

  1. Round 1: Zyxxal uses Tunnel of "Love" to split the party.
  2. Round 2: Doc Phantasm offers Fey Dust to the wizard.
  3. Round 3: Carousel of Flesh grinds down the fighter.
  4. Round 4: Zyxxal Critical Backstabs the addicted rogue.

Final Notes

  • For Players: A battle of wits, perception checks, and resisting temptation.
  • For DMs: Play Zyxxal as unpredictable—he might spare a PC who amuses him, or curse the one who bores him.
  • Treasure: The carnival's "prizes" are all cursed (e.g., a teddy bear that whispers secrets, a ring that swaps genders daily).

Need even more chaos? Add a hallucinogenic fog that randomizes spell effects, or a "second boss" that's just Zyxxal's illusion.
