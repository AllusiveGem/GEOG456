3D Printed Models & Maps

Explore my collection of 3D printed urban models, architectural prototypes, and interactive maps. Each piece combines technical precision with creative design to visualize urban spaces and concepts.

Urban Development Model

A detailed 3D model showcasing a sustainable urban development project. This model highlights green spaces, efficient transportation systems, and mixed-use zoning to demonstrate how cities can balance growth with environmental responsibility.

Scale: 1:1000 2024

Architectural Prototype

This prototype explores innovative use of materials and space in modern architecture. The design incorporates parametric elements and sustainable building techniques, printed at 1:200 scale to test structural concepts.

Scale: 1:200 2023

Historical Building Reconstruction

A faithful 3D reconstruction of a 19th-century building, created from archival photographs and architectural drawings. This model preserves intricate facade details that were at risk of being lost to time.

Scale: 1:150 2023

Topographical City Map

An interactive 3D-printed map showing urban topography and infrastructure. This tactile representation helps planners and students understand elevation changes and their impact on urban development.

Scale: 1:2500 2024
